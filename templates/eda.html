📊 Exploratory Visualizations & Statistical Patterns

6 Comprehensive Plots

1. Target Class Frequency & Proportions

Balanced class constitutes 37.5%, Savers 32.9%, and High-Spenders 29.6%, providing a well-balanced dataset free from severe class imbalance bias.

2. Key Financial Ratios by Archetype

Boxplots show clear separation across Savings Rate, Expense-to-Income, DTI, and Discretionary Spend. High-Spenders consistently cluster in the danger zone (>90% expense ratio, >35% discretionary spend).

3. Category-Wise Expense Composition

Savers allocate >75% of their total spending to essential categories (housing, groceries, healthcare). High-Spenders allocate nearly 40% to discretionary shopping and entertainment.

4. Feature Correlation Matrix

Strong negative correlation (-0.89) between Savings Rate and Expense-to-Income Ratio. High positive correlation (+0.78) between Credit Card Utilization and Debt-to-Income Ratio.

5. Monthly Income vs Monthly Expenses

Scatter plot with the 45-degree break-even line shows High-Spenders consistently touching or exceeding the break-even diagonal, indicating living paycheck-to-paycheck regardless of income level.

6. Discretionary Spending vs. Savings Rate

Demonstrates distinct behavioral clustering: Savers cluster in the upper-left quadrant (high savings, low discretionary), whereas High-Spenders dominate the lower-right quadrant.
